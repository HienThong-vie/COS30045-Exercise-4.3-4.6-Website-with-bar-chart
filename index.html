Televisions on the Australian market

Which brands register the most TV models? This chart replaces the static KNIME image on my Week 0–3 Appliance Energy website with a chart drawn by D3 from a CSV file (Exercises 4.3 to 4.7).

Samsung, Kogan and LG register the most TV models

Number of TV models registered per brand (brands with 20 or more models), sorted from most to fewest. The dashed line shows the average.

Data: Australian Government Energy Rating TV registrations (Feb 2026 extract), grouped by brand in KNIME and exported as data/tvBrandCount.csv.
